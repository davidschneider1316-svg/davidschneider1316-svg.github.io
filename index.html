Conventional Loans

Standard financing for buyers with stable credit. 3% down for first-time buyers, 5% for others. No upfront MI and cancellable PMI once you reach 20% equity.

FHA Loans

Government-backed with 3.5% down. Flexible credit requirements and higher DTI allowances. Ideal for buyers with lower credit scores or limited down payment funds.

VA Loans

100% financing for Veterans and Active Duty. No monthly PMI, lower interest rates, and limited closing costs. One of the strongest benefits of military service.

USDA Loans

Zero down payment for homes in rural-designated Indiana areas. Competitive rates and lower mortgage insurance than FHA. Income and location limits apply.

Renovation Loans

Includes repair costs in your purchase loan. Perfect for turning a fixer-upper into your dream home with one single closing and one monthly payment.
